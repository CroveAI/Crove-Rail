Building meaningful customer relationships shouldn't be this hard.

Every day, businesses struggle to manage conversations across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and countless other channels. Messages get lost. Customers wait. Teams feel overwhelmed. We knew there had to be a better way.

## 1. Why Crove, Why Now

After years of watching businesses juggle multiple messaging platforms, losing track of customer conversations, and missing opportunities to build lasting relationships, we decided to build something different. 

Crove is born from a simple belief: **every customer conversation matters**, and businesses deserve tools that make managing those conversations effortless, not exhausting.

The explosion of messaging channels has created both an opportunity and a challenge. Customers expect instant, personalized responses wherever they reach out. But for businesses, keeping up has become nearly impossible. Until now.
